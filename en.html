Phocal Privacy Policy · Summary

Photo desktop widget tool · Local-first
中文
Last updated: September 1, 2026

Core Principle

Phocal is a “local-first” app: all of your boards, photos, and stickers are stored only on your device by default. We do not collect, upload, or store your personal information.

1. Data Storage

  • All boards, photos, stickers and other data you create are stored on your device by default
  • We do not proactively collect, upload, or store any personal information on our servers
  • Widget data is shared between the main app and the widget extension via the iOS App Group, and remains stored locally on your device
  • You can export all of your data as a file via the “Data Backup” feature; the exported file is kept by you

2. Information Collection (Minimized)

  • We do not collect: name, phone number, email, or other personal identity information; location; or your contacts
  • We never upload your photos or stickers to cloud servers
  • Device permissions (for local features only, fully under your control and switchable anytime): Photo Library (to pick photos), Camera (to take new photos)
  • We may collect only necessary anonymous device information to improve stability: crash logs, device model and OS version (no personal data)

3. Data Security

  • Your data is stored in your device's sandbox; other apps cannot access it
  • Your data is protected by iOS system security mechanisms; the widget only shares data via App Group for this app's internal use
  • When switching devices, migrate data manually via “Data Backup”; uninstalling the app deletes all local data, so please back up in advance

4. Third-Party Services

  • Subscriptions are processed through Apple StoreKit; payment information is handled by Apple, and we cannot access your payment details
  • WidgetKit (desktop widgets) and the Vision framework (photo cutout) run entirely on-device and upload no data

5. Your Rights

  • View / edit / delete: you can view, edit or delete any photo, sticker, or board at any time
  • Export: you can export your complete data via the “Data Backup” feature
  • Choice: you are free to choose whether to use features that require permissions

6. Protection of Minors

  • Suitable for users of all ages; we do not proactively collect any user's personal information
  • Users under the age of 14 are recommended to use the app under a guardian's guidance

7. Policy Updates

  • If we update this policy, it will be posted in the app with the update date; local data is not affected by policy updates
